# Process

I used ChatGPT throughout the writing process to help me understand the assignment, narrow the topic, organise the argument, improve the English, and revise the structure of the essay.

One idea I kept was the distinction between **writing code** and **judging code**. I found this useful because it matched my own experience in Unity. AI can generate a script quickly, but I still need to understand whether the logic fits the interaction I designed and whether the result actually works for the experience.

One idea I rejected was the broader argument that, because AI lowers the barrier to coding, creativity will simply become more important. Although I still agree with part of this idea, I felt it was too general for this assignment. The brief asked for a more specific argument grounded in my own practice, so I narrowed the essay to a smaller question: if AI can write the code, why do I still need to understand programming?

I also rejected an earlier structure with several section headings because it made the essay feel too much like an outline. I removed most of the headings and reorganised the paragraphs so the argument could develop more naturally from the question, to my Unity experience, and finally to my conclusion.

ChatGPT also helped me revise the English expression and check the flow, but the central argument and the project example came from my own experience.
